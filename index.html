A passion for traditional jazz is at the heart of Sweet Georgia Brown. Inspired by the golden age of Dixieland and early swing, the duo brings the timeless sound of jazz to life with energy, elegance, and authenticity.

The repertoire includes beloved jazz standards and songs made famous by legendary artists such as Louis Armstrong, Jelly Roll Morton, Fats Waller, Bix Beiderbecke, Ella Fitzgerald, Duke Ellington, and many others. The unique sound of Sweet Georgia Brown is enriched by the warm and expressive voice of Andrea Bruno and Luca Costantini’s soft and bright guitar

Natural style and old-soul charm bring depth and emotion to every performance. Whether performing on stage or at a private event, the duo creates an elegant and welcoming atmosphere where guests can relax, dream, and enjoy the timeless spirit of swing.
