Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This page explains how personal data may be handled when you contact Sweet Georgia Brown through this website.

Data controller

Andrea Bruno
Email: andreabrunoblues@gmail.com

Data we receive

The website does not send contact-form entries to a database or store them on its server. Until you submit, the entries remain in your browser. If you choose to send the enquiry, your default email application creates a message containing the information you entered, which may include your first name, last name, email address, event type, approximate dates and message.

Why we use the data

We use these details only to reply to enquiries, evaluate performance proposals and communicate about a possible booking. The legal basis is taking steps at the request of the person before entering into a contract, or pursuing the legitimate interest of replying to a direct enquiry.

How the contact form works

The form opens the default email application on your device using a mailto link. The website does not transmit or store the entries itself. The message is sent to andreabrunoblues@gmail.com only when you confirm it in your email application. After sending, the information is handled in the recipient email account and may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to manage the enquiry or any resulting professional relationship.

Retention and recipients

Enquiry data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the request and any resulting professional relationship, or to meet legal obligations. The data may be handled by the email service used to send and receive the message.

Cookies and external services

This website does not intentionally use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts and may load a technical JavaScript library from jsDelivr; those providers may process connection data according to their own policies. Links to services such as Spotify, YouTube and social networks open third-party websites, which have separate privacy practices.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Contact us at andreabrunoblues@gmail.com.

Important note

This is a practical website draft. Before publication, the controller’s full legal identity and postal address should be added and the wording checked against the actual hosting, email and font configuration.
